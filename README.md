# Global-Superstore-Sales-Customer-Intelligence-Analysis-Using-Python
The Global Superstore Sales &amp; Customer Intelligence Analytics Using Python project focuses on analyzing global retail sales data to identify business trends, evaluate product performance, understand customer purchasing behaviours, and improve business decision-making.
# Project Overview
- The Global Superstore Sales & Customer Intelligence Analytics Using Python is an end-to-end data analytics project focused on analyzing global retail transaction data to evaluate business performance and gain meaningful insights into sales, profitability, and customer behaviour.

- This project uses Python-based data analysis techniques to explore sales transactions across different countries, regions, product categories, and customer segments. It aims to identify sales trends, understand purchasing patterns, evaluate product performance, and discover factors affecting business profitability.

# 🎯 Project Objectives
- **Sales Analysis:** Analyze overall sales performance, revenue trends, and monthly and yearly sales growth.
- **Product Analysis:** Identify top-selling products, high-revenue products, and low-performing product categories.
- **Customer Analysis:** Understand customer purchasing behaviour, purchase frequency, and spending patterns.
- **Customer Segmentation:** Apply RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) analysis to classify customers into meaningful segments based on      their purchasing behaviour.
- **Profit Analysis:** Evaluate overall profitability and identify profitable and loss-making products, categories, and sub-categories.
- **Discount Analysis:** Examine the relationship between discount levels, sales, and profit margins to understand their impact on         business   profitability.
- **Regional Analysis:** Compare sales and profitability across different countries, regions, and markets to identify high-performing       and low-performing areas.
- **Shipping Analysis:** Analyze shipping modes, shipping costs, and delivery time to evaluate shipping performance.
- **Return Analysis:** Identify returned orders and analyze return patterns across different regions and business segments.
